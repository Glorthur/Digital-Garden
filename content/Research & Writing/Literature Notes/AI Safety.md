# AI Safety

## Definition

The field and policy frame concerned with reducing the risk of harmful behavior, misuse, loss of control, or dangerous deployment of advanced AI systems.

## Why It Matters

[[AI Safety]] is not just a moral wrapper in the Glasswing discussion. It is the explicit justification for keeping a powerful system restricted rather than publicly released.

## Evidence / Notes

- Safety can function as genuine risk management and as a gatekeeping mechanism.
- The interesting question is not whether safety matters, but who gets to define safe use and who gets excluded.
