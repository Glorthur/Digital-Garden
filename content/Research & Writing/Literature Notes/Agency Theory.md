# Agency Theory

## Definition

A theory explaining conflicts between principals and agents, especially between owners and managers.

## Why It Matters

It provides the main logic for viewing boards as monitoring devices that reduce opportunism and align interests.

## Evidence / Notes

- Frequently used to justify smaller, tighter, more disciplined boards.
- Central to arguments around CEO duality, incentives, and board oversight.
