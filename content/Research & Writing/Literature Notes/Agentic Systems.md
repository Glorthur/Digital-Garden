# Agentic Systems

## Definition

AI systems that act across multiple steps, pursue goals, and carry out complex workflows with limited direct supervision.

## Why It Matters

The practical fear in the Glasswing case is not just that a model can answer security questions. It is that a capable system can autonomously discover, test, and exploit vulnerabilities at meaningful scale.

## Evidence / Notes

- Agentic capacity changes the risk profile of a model.
- The stronger the autonomy, the weaker the case for broad public release when the domain is offensive-capable.
