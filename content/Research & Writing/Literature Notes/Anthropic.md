# Anthropic

## Definition

An AI lab and company focused on frontier model development, safety framing, and enterprise deployment.

## Why It Matters

[[Anthropic]] is the institution behind [[Project Glasswing]], which makes it central to any analysis of restricted frontier capability, controlled deployment, and AI governance.

## Evidence / Notes

- Publicly positioned around safety and controlled deployment.
- Strategically relevant not just for consumer models, but for what it appears to keep private.
- The Glasswing case suggests Anthropic may be using safety constraints as both governance logic and competitive positioning.
