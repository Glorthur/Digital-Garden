# Board Composition

## Definition

The structure and makeup of the board, including size, independence, diversity, and leadership arrangement.

## Why It Matters

It is the core explanatory variable across much of the governance-performance literature.

## Evidence / Notes

- Main sub-dimensions include [[Board Size]], [[Board Independence]], and [[Gender Diversity]].
- Empirical findings are mixed globally and in [[Ghana]].
