# Board Independence

## Definition

The extent to which outside or non-executive directors are represented on the board.

## Why It Matters

It is commonly treated as a governance-quality indicator, though the performance evidence is inconsistent.

## Evidence / Notes

- Independence is often assumed to improve monitoring.
- The literature repeatedly shows that more independent boards do not automatically translate into better performance.
