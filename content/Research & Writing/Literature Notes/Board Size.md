# Board Size

## Definition

The number of directors serving on a firm's board.

## Why It Matters

It is frequently used as a proxy for coordination quality, monitoring capacity, and access to expertise.

## Evidence / Notes

- Smaller boards are often argued to perform better because of lower coordination costs.
- Some studies find positive or insignificant effects, especially in context-specific settings.
