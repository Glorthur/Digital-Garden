# Corporate Governance

## Definition

The system of rules, oversight structures, board arrangements, and control mechanisms through which firms are directed and monitored.

## Why It Matters

It provides the broader frame for debates about [[Board Composition]], board monitoring, accountability, and firm performance.

## Evidence / Notes

- Often linked to agency problems, monitoring quality, sustainability, audit quality, and shareholder protection.
- In the Ghana-focused literature, findings are mixed and often context-dependent.
