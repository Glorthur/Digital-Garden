# Cybersecurity

## Definition

The practice of identifying, preventing, mitigating, and responding to digital threats, vulnerabilities, and system compromise.

## Why It Matters

In the Glasswing context, [[Cybersecurity]] is the concrete domain where frontier AI may produce both its clearest benefits and its most dangerous misuse cases.

## Evidence / Notes

- The line between defensive and offensive capability is thin.
- Vulnerability discovery tools can be socially useful or catastrophically abusable depending on access and control.
