# Gender Diversity

## Definition

The representation of women on corporate boards and leadership structures.

## Why It Matters

It is one of the most discussed board-composition variables in the recent governance literature and one of the more promising lines in the Ghana-focused evidence.

## Evidence / Notes

- Often associated with stronger monitoring and better attendance.
- Effects on firm performance are positive in many studies but not uniform.
