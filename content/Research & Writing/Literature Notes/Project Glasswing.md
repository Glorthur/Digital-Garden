# Project Glasswing

## Definition

A reported Anthropic-led defensive cybersecurity initiative built around a restricted model called "Claude Mythos preview."

## Why It Matters

[[Project Glasswing]] is one of the clearest examples in this vault of a frontier AI capability being kept out of public hands and deployed through institutional partnerships instead.

## Evidence / Notes

- Described as an invitation-only defensive cybersecurity coalition.
- Framed around identifying serious vulnerabilities before malicious actors exploit them.
- Important less as a brand and more as a signal about restricted model deployment.
