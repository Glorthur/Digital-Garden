# Resource Dependence Theory

## Definition

A theory that views boards as providers of external resources, legitimacy, and strategic access rather than only internal monitors.

## Why It Matters

It helps explain why larger or more diverse boards may be beneficial in some environments.

## Evidence / Notes

- Useful for interpreting board composition in relation to institutional pressures, expertise, and external dependence.
- A strong counterweight to narrow monitoring-based interpretations.
