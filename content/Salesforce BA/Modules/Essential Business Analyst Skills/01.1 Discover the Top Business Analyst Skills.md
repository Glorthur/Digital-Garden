# 01.1 Discover the Top Business Analyst Skills

- Unit: Discover the Top Business Analyst Skills

## Summary (Max 5 bullets)
- The BA's primary role is to help enable change that improves business outcomes.
- BAs combine business process understanding with technology understanding to recommend the right solution.
- Core skill area 1: information discovery (gathering complete, decision-quality information).
- Core skill area 2: analysis and synthesis (interpreting details and patterns to shape plans and decisions).
- Core skill area 3: communication, collaboration, and documentation to align stakeholders and teams.

## Key Terms
- `Enable change`: Drive improvements in process or systems to meet organizational goals.
- `Information discovery`: Systematic collection of project-relevant facts from people, systems, and process context.
- `Analysis and synthesis`: Evaluate data, connect findings, and translate them into actionable recommendations.
- `Collaboration`: Cross-functional alignment among business stakeholders, delivery teams, and project leadership.

## Frameworks / Methods
- BA core skills model:
  - Information discovery
  - Analysis and synthesis
  - Communication, collaboration, documentation
- Change framing checklist:
  - What is the business goal?
  - What process/system needs improvement?
  - Who is impacted?

## Salesforce-Specific Example
- Sales leadership needs forecast adjustments in Salesforce. The BA clarifies business need, maps current process, identifies stakeholders (VP Sales, managers, reps), and translates requirements into a workable Salesforce solution with clear documentation.
