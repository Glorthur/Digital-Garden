# 01.2 Explore Techniques for Information Discovery

- Module: Essential Business Analyst Skills
- Unit: Explore Techniques for Information Discovery

## Summary (Max 5 bullets)
- Information discovery starts with broad questioning: who, what, where, why, when, and how.
- Project information can be grouped into project history, analysis, and elicitation.
- Key analysis types include enterprise analysis, strategy/gap analysis, and stakeholder analysis.
- Elicitation is central BA work because many requirements are undocumented and live in stakeholder knowledge.
- Elicitation is iterative: prepare, conduct, confirm results, then refine as needed across the project lifecycle.

## Key Terms
- `Elicitation`: Drawing out requirements and design information from stakeholders and other sources.
- `Enterprise analysis`: Understanding organizational structure, reporting lines, and departmental interactions.
- `Strategy analysis`: Comparing current state vs future state and evaluating options to close gaps.
- `Stakeholder analysis`: Identifying who is impacted, influential, and necessary for requirement definition.
- `Gap analysis`: Defining the delta between current and desired states.

## Frameworks / Methods
- 6-question discovery lens: `Who`, `What`, `Where`, `Why`, `When`, `How`.
- Elicitation techniques noted in unit:
  - Brainstorming
  - Document analysis
  - Focus groups
  - Interface analysis
  - Interviews
  - Observation
  - Requirements workshops
  - Surveys/questionnaires
- Elicitation stages:
  - Prepare
  - Conduct
  - Confirm results

## Salesforce-Specific Example
- For forecast visibility improvements, the BA asks 6-question prompts, maps current-state forecasting workflow, runs interviews/workshops with sales stakeholders, validates findings, and converts confirmed needs into Salesforce requirements.
