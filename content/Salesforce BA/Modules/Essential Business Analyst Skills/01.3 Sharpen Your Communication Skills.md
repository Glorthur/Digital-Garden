# 01.3 Sharpen Your Communication Skills

## Unit
- Module: Essential Business Analyst Skills
- Unit: Sharpen Your Communication Skills
- Trailhead URL: https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/business-analyst_skills-strategies/sharpen-your-communication-skills
- Date: 2026-04-07

## Summary (Max 5 bullets)
- Communication is required at every stage of the project lifecycle, not only at handoff points.
- BA communication supports elicitation, stakeholder alignment, and solution delivery.
- Stakeholder engagement improves when you ask clear questions, listen actively, and respect time constraints.
- Four communication modes matter for BAs: verbal, nonverbal, written, and visual.
- Communication quality can determine project success or failure.

## Key Terms
- `Active listening`: Intentional listening to understand meaning and intent before responding.
- `Verbal communication`: Spoken communication in meetings, interviews, and presentations.
- `Nonverbal communication`: Meaning conveyed by posture, gestures, facial expression, and eye contact.
- `Written communication`: Durable record through docs, email, chat, and requirements artifacts.
- `Visual communication`: Charts, diagrams, wireframes, and visuals that improve understanding.

## Frameworks / Methods
- Stakeholder engagement tips from unit:
  - Ask questions and listen
  - Demonstrate value early
  - Respect stakeholder time and clarify next steps
  - Identify root causes of tension and adapt
- Communication mode checklist:
  - Verbal: clarity and audience-fit tone
  - Nonverbal: body language and cues
  - Written: concise, professional, reviewed
  - Visual: relevant and simplifying

## Salesforce-Specific Example
- During requirements sessions for a Salesforce enhancement, the BA uses verbal questioning and active listening, reads nonverbal cues in workshops, documents decisions in writing, and uses visuals (process maps/wireframes) to secure alignment.
